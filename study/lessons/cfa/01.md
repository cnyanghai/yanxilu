# CFA 第 1 课 · Q1 收益率与回报（1/4）

> 35 分钟 · CFA 一级第 1 / 64 课 · Rates and Returns

**模块**：Quantitative Methods → Rates and Returns（共 4 次课，今天第 1 次）

## 学习目标

- 从三个角度解释利率：要求回报率、折现率、机会成本。
- 把利率拆成实际无风险利率加各项溢价。
- 计算单期和多期的持有期收益率（HPR）。

## 讲解一：利率的三种解读

假设一年期定期存款利率是 4%：

1. **Required rate of return（要求回报率）**：你把 1 万元存一年，至少要 4% 才愿意——这是你“要求”的最低回报。
2. **Discount rate（折现率）**：一年后到手的 10,400 元，按 4% 折回今天，值 10,000 元。把“未来的钱”换算成“今天的钱”。
3. **Opportunity cost（机会成本）**：如果你把这 1 万元放在不计息的活期里，就放弃了 4%——这就是你为“随时能用钱”付出的代价。

做题看关键词：*minimum / require* → 要求回报率；*worth today / present value* → 折现率；*forgo / give up / sacrifice* → 机会成本。

## 讲解二：利率的构成

$$
r = \underbrace{\text{实际无风险利率} + \text{通胀溢价}}_{\text{名义无风险利率}} + \text{违约风险溢价} + \text{流动性溢价} + \text{期限溢价}
$$

一个记法：出借人问自己五个问题——

| 问题 | 对应部分 |
|---|---|
| 我得等多久才能花这笔钱？（纯等待） | Real risk-free interest rate |
| 等的这段时间，钱会不会贬值？ | Inflation premium |
| 对方会不会不还？ | Default risk premium |
| 我想提前卖掉时，能不能按公道价卖出去？ | Liquidity premium |
| 期限越长，利率一变价格晃得越厉害，我愿不愿扛？ | Maturity premium |

短期国库券（T-bill）只回答了前两个问题（后三项接近 0），所以它的收益率近似**名义无风险利率**。严格关系是 (1 + 名义) = (1 + 实际)(1 + 通胀)，一级题目一般直接相加近似。

**例题 1**：实际无风险利率 2.5%，预期通胀 3%。某 15 年期公司债：违约风险溢价 1.8%，流动性溢价 0.7%，期限溢价 1.2%。

- 名义无风险利率 ≈ 2.5% + 3% = **5.5%**（严格：1.025 × 1.03 − 1 = 5.575%）
- 公司债要求回报率 ≈ 5.5% + 1.8% + 0.7% + 1.2% = **9.2%**

**例题 2**：同一家公司又发了一只 15 年期债券，条款一样，但交易非常活跃，要求回报率是 8.7%。哪一项溢价不同？差多少？

- 发行人、期限都一样，违约和期限溢价相同；差别只能来自流动性：9.2% − 8.7% = **0.5%**，活跃债券的流动性溢价只有 0.2%。

## 讲解三：持有期收益率（HPR）

**单期**：

$$
HPR = \frac{P_1 - P_0 + D_1}{P_0} = \underbrace{\frac{P_1 - P_0}{P_0}}_{\text{资本利得率}} + \underbrace{\frac{D_1}{P_0}}_{\text{收入回报率}}
$$

**例题 3（股票）**：60 元买入，持有期间分红 1.8 元，以 63.2 元卖出。

HPR = (63.2 − 60 + 1.8) ÷ 60 = 5 ÷ 60 = **8.33%**（资本利得 5.33% + 股息收益 3%）

计算器（BA II Plus）：`63.2 − 60 + 1.8 =` → `÷ 60 =` → 显示 `0.0833`

**例题 4（债券）**：面值 100 的债券按 100 买入，一年收到票息 4，年末以 106.5 卖出。

HPR = (106.5 − 100 + 4) ÷ 100 = **10.5%**——票息只有 4%，另外 6.5% 来自价格上涨（比如期间市场利率下降）。

**多期**：每期“1 + 收益率”连乘，再减 1。

$$
HPR = (1 + R_1)(1 + R_2)\cdots(1 + R_n) - 1
$$

**例题 5**：某基金三年收益率 15%、−12%、6%。

(1.15)(0.88)(1.06) − 1 = 1.07272 − 1 = **7.27%**

计算器：`1.15 × 0.88 × 1.06 − 1 =` → 显示 `0.0727`

直觉检查：只看前两年，涨 15% 再跌 12%，1.15 × 0.88 = 1.012，只赚了 1.2%，不是 3%。跌的那 12% 是从更高的基数上跌的——这就是不能直接相加的原因。

## 练习：仿一级考试三选一

**1.** "To find what a payment of $1,000 due in two years is worth today, the analyst uses a rate of 7%." The 7% is best described as a:

A. discount rate.
B. required rate of return.
C. opportunity cost.

**2.** Which premium compensates investors for the increased sensitivity of a bond's market value to changes in interest rates as its time to repayment is extended?

A. Liquidity premium
B. Maturity premium
C. Inflation premium

**3.** The nominal risk-free rate is 5.2%, and the real risk-free rate is 2.0%. Using the additive approximation, the inflation premium is closest to:

A. 2.0%
B. 3.2%
C. 7.2%

**4.** An investor buys a share at $20.00, receives a dividend of $0.60, and sells the share at $18.40. The holding period return is closest to:

A. −8.0%
B. −5.0%
C. 3.0%

**5.** A portfolio earns 7%, 12%, −5%, and 4% in four consecutive years. The four-year holding period return is closest to:

A. 18.00%
B. 18.40%
C. 4.60%

<details>
<summary>CFA 答案与解析</summary>

1. **A**。关键词 *worth today*——把未来的钱换算到今天，这是折现率的角度。
2. **B**。期限越长，利率变动对价格的影响越大，补偿这一点的是期限溢价。流动性溢价补偿“不好卖”，通胀溢价补偿购买力下降。
3. **B**。5.2% − 2.0% = 3.2%。C 是把两者相加的错误。
4. **B**。(18.40 − 20.00 + 0.60) ÷ 20.00 = −1.00 ÷ 20 = −5.0%。A 漏了股息（−1.60 ÷ 20 = −8%）；C 只算了股息收益率（0.60 ÷ 20 = 3%）。
5. **B**。1.07 × 1.12 × 0.95 × 1.04 − 1 = 1.18402 − 1 = 18.40%。A 是直接相加；C 是把 18.40% 除以 4——年均收益率要用几何平均（开四次方），第 2 次课讲。

</details>

## 加练一

> 同一课另一套仿真题，正课的练习做完还有余力再做。

**1.** An analyst states: "The 6% rate is the minimum return investors require before they agree to lend." This statement best describes the interest rate as a:

A. discount rate.
B. required rate of return.
C. opportunity cost.

**2.** Which component of an interest rate compensates investors for the risk that a security cannot be sold quickly at close to its fair value?

A. Maturity premium
B. Default risk premium
C. Liquidity premium

**3.** Given: real risk-free rate 1.5%, inflation premium 2.5%, default risk premium 2.0%, liquidity premium 0.8%, maturity premium 1.2%. The required return on the bond is closest to:

A. 6.0%
B. 7.2%
C. 8.0%

**4.** An investor buys a share at $40, receives a dividend of $0.80, and sells the share at $37. The holding period return is closest to:

A. −7.5%
B. −5.5%
C. −2.0%

**5.** A portfolio returns 12%, −8%, and 5% in three consecutive years. The three-year holding period return is closest to:

A. 9.00%
B. 8.19%
C. 2.73%

<details>
<summary>CFA 答案与解析</summary>

1. **B**。关键词 *minimum return investors require*。折现率强调把未来价值换算到今天；机会成本强调放弃的收益。
2. **C**。流动性溢价补偿“难以快速按公允价卖出”。期限溢价补偿长期限下的利率风险；违约风险溢价补偿可能不还钱。
3. **C**。1.5 + 2.5 + 2.0 + 0.8 + 1.2 = 8.0%。A 只加了前三项，漏了流动性和期限溢价（1.5 + 2.5 + 2.0 = 6.0%）。
4. **B**。(37 − 40 + 0.80) ÷ 40 = −2.20 ÷ 40 = −5.5%。A 漏掉了股息（−3 ÷ 40 = −7.5%）。
5. **B**。1.12 × 0.92 × 1.05 − 1 = 1.08192 − 1 = 8.19%。A 是直接相加的错误做法；C 是 8.19% 除以 3 的错误“年均”做法（年均收益率要开三次方，第 2 次课讲几何平均）。

</details>

## 加练二

> 同一课另一套仿真题，正课的练习做完还有余力再做。

**1.** "By keeping $10,000 in a non-interest-bearing account, Wen gives up the 3% she could earn on a one-year deposit." The 3% is best described as a(n):

A. discount rate.
B. opportunity cost.
C. required rate of return.

**2.** The nominal risk-free interest rate is best approximated by the sum of the:

A. real risk-free rate and the inflation premium.
B. real risk-free rate and the default risk premium.
C. inflation premium and the maturity premium.

**3.** A 3-month Treasury bill yields 4.6%, and the real risk-free rate is 1.9%. The inflation premium is closest to:

A. 1.9%
B. 2.7%
C. 6.5%

**4.** An investor buys a share for $25.00, receives a dividend of $0.50, and sells the share for $27.50 one year later. The holding period return is closest to:

A. 2.0%
B. 10.0%
C. 12.0%

**5.** A fund returns 6%, 9%, and −4% in three consecutive years. The three-year holding period return is closest to:

A. 3.64%
B. 10.92%
C. 11.00%

<details>
<summary>CFA 答案与解析</summary>

1. **B**。关键词 *gives up*：放弃了本可以赚到的 3%，是机会成本。没有“最低要求”的意思（排除 C），也没有把未来价值折到今天（排除 A）。
2. **A**。名义无风险利率 = 实际无风险利率 + 通胀溢价。违约、流动性、期限溢价是具体证券才有的风险补偿，无风险利率里不含。
3. **B**。T-bill 收益率近似名义无风险利率：4.6% − 1.9% = 2.7%。C 是错误地把两者相加。
4. **C**。(27.50 − 25.00 + 0.50) ÷ 25.00 = 3.00 ÷ 25.00 = 12.0%。B 漏掉了股息（2.50 ÷ 25 = 10%）；A 只算了股息收益率（0.50 ÷ 25 = 2%）。
5. **B**。1.06 × 1.09 × 0.96 − 1 = 1.1092 − 1 = 10.92%。C 是直接相加（6 + 9 − 4 = 11%）；A 是把 10.92% 除以 3 的错误“年均”算法——年均收益率要用几何平均开三次方，第 2 次课讲。

</details>

### 短文阅读

**What Is an Interest Rate?**

An interest rate can be read in three ways. First, it is a required rate of return: the minimum return an investor needs before agreeing to lend money. Second, it is a discount rate, which tells us how much a payment in the future is worth today. Third, it is an opportunity cost, because money spent today gives up the interest it could have earned.

Interest rates are also built from several parts. The real risk-free interest rate rewards investors simply for waiting. An inflation premium protects them against rising prices. Riskier securities add more premiums: a default risk premium if the borrower might not pay, a liquidity premium if the security is hard to sell quickly at a fair price, and a maturity premium if the money is tied up for many years.

After an investment is made, we measure the result with the holding period return, which counts both the price change and any income received.

**理解题**

1. According to the passage, what are the three ways to read an interest rate?
2. Which premium is added when a security is hard to sell quickly at a fair price?
3. What two things does the holding period return count?

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

1. A required rate of return, a discount rate, and an opportunity cost.（要求回报率、折现率、机会成本）
2. The liquidity premium.（流动性溢价）
3. The price change and any income received (e.g., dividends or interest).（价格变动和期间收到的收入）

</details>
